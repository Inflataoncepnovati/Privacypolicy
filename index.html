The significance of creating an app for inflatable sensing objects is: to make environmental states, object states, and spatial relationships perceptible, touchable, and responsive; to turn everyday objects from passive tools into expressive environmental interfaces; and to develop “inflation” from a physical phenomenon into a designable, narratable, and critically engaging object media language—using a “multi-sensing—translation—inflatable form—relational system” approach to generate new object experiences and spatial relationships.
